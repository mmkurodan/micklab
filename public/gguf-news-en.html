Latest GGUF Model News

A daily digest of new and updated models available as GGUF, plus related updates to llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio and more. Follow the source links for the primary information. Dates are in JST.

    8B (4B effective) Q4_K_M NVIDIA Unsloth

    Unsloth publishes GGUF of NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B

    A compact NVIDIA model built on the Gemma-4-E4B architecture: 8B total parameters (4B effective), about 4GB at Q4_K_M, up to 64K tokens in and out, with thinking mode toggled via the chat template. Licensed under OpenMDW-1.1.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/unsloth/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-GGUF

    9B Q8_0〜Q3_K_L bartowski

    bartowski releases GGUF of MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B

    Quantized builds of a 9B Qwen-based distilled model, from Q8_0 (about 9.6GB) down to Q3_K_L; the common Q4_K_S / Q4_0 files are about 5.5GB.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/bartowski/MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B-GGUF

    4B / 9B vision inclusionAI bartowski

    GGUF builds of inclusionAI's vision models ZoomGain 4B / 9B

    inclusionAI released the image-text models ZoomGain-4B / 9B with official GGUF builds; bartowski also offers versions with 21 quantization types.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/collections/inclusionAI/zoomgain

    197M F16 / Q8_0 EN/RU

    Nano-1.3-Plus: a tiny 197M English/Russian chat model in GGUF

    Released November 15: a 197M-parameter model with 4,096-token context. F16 is about 512MB and Q8_0 about 274MB, small enough for phones or a Raspberry Pi 5. Meant for simple short chats; unreliable on facts and arithmetic.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/LGTEAMai/Nano-1.3-Plus-GGUF

    llama.cpp 1.0.0 ggml

    llama.cpp and ggml reach 1.0.0

    llama.cpp and ggml 1.0.0 were released on November 13, with API/ABI breaks to be signaled by major-version bumps from now on. Tags starting with b remain nightly builds; official releases use semantic-version tags.

    Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/release.md
