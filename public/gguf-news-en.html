Latest GGUF Model News

A daily digest of new and updated models available as GGUF, plus related updates to llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio and more. Follow the source links for the primary information. Dates are in JST.

    26.4B MoE (3.4B active) UD-IQ1_S〜UD-Q8_K_XL multimodal Unsloth

    Unsloth publishes GGUF quants of Inkling 2 Mini (Thinking Machines Lab)

    Unsloth released Dynamic-quant GGUFs of Inkling 2 Mini, which Thinking Machines Lab published on Nov 20 under Apache-2.0 (official card: 26.4B total / 3.4B active MoE). It accepts image input and supports up to 1M-token context. Quants range from UD-IQ1_S (~7.8 GB) to UD-Q8_K_XL (~22.9 GB); UD-Q4_K_M is ~16.6 GB.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Inkling-2-Mini-GGUF

    llama.cpp OpenCL Vulkan

    llama.cpp b12464–b12555 (Nov 17–19): OpenCL and Vulkan improvements

    b12555 (Nov 19) lets OpenCL's decomposed flash attention use int8-quantized KQ ops; b12499 (Nov 18) makes Vulkan RMS norm use a runtime loop count on Qualcomm; b12464 (Nov 17) adds an OpenCL Q8_0 kernel for a specific path. No new model architecture support appears in this range.

    Source: https://freedom.tech/project/llama-cpp/

    321B 4B / 9B bartowski

    bartowski updates GGUFs for Nex-N2.6 (321B) and ZoomGain 4B/9B

    In bartowski's "Latest from known labs" collection, Nex-N2.6-GGUF (321.3B, 24 quant variants) and ZoomGain-4B / 9B GGUFs were updated within roughly the last day. The page shows only relative times, so exact dates are unconfirmed.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/collections/bartowski/latest-from-known-labs

    Q4NX AMD NPU Unsloth

    Unsloth v0.1.920-beta adds Q4NX export for AMD NPUs

    The Nov 19 release adds Q4NX conversion on the Export page so models can load on AMD NPUs, plus LTX-2.5 / MOSS-Image LoRA training and up to 25% faster QLoRA. This release itself has no GGUF-specific changes.

    Source: https://unsloth.ai/docs/new/changelog

    LM Studio llama.cpp bug fix

    LM Studio Bionic 1.2.1: llama.cpp extension packs and a GGUF GPU-offload fix

    Bionic 1.2.1 (Nov 16) ships llama.cpp extension packs 2.74.0 / 2.76.0 and fixes wrong GPU-offload settings for GGUF models with large metadata headers. 2.76.0 is LM Studio's pack version, not an upstream llama.cpp build number.

    Source: https://lmstudio.ai/changelog/bionic-v1.2.1

    Ollama llama.cpp b12257 prerelease

    Ollama v0.40.17 still in prerelease (rc1); bundled llama.cpp bumped to b12257

    v0.40.17 remains in prerelease (rc0 on Nov 10, rc1 on Nov 16), with the bundled llama.cpp bumped to b12257, concurrent MLX generation and Vulkan iGPU mmap. The current stable release is v0.40.16, which added Ripple Preview (65B).

    Source: https://releasebot.io/updates/ollama
