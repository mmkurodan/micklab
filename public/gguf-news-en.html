Latest GGUF Model News

A daily digest of new and updated models available as GGUF, plus related updates to llama.cpp, Ollama, LM Studio and more. Follow the source links for the primary information. Dates are in JST.

    2 items
    llama.cpp CPU b12620

    llama.cpp b12620: better memory access pattern in CPU matmul

    Reworks how the ggml CPU backend accesses memory in the mat-mul chunk routine (ggml_compute_forward_mul_mat_one_chunk), PR #30846. Published as a pre-release build.

    Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b12620

    llama.cpp SYCL IQ4_XS b12618

    llama.cpp b12618: SYCL weight reorder for IQ4_NL and IQ4_XS

    Adds weight reordering for IQ4_NL and IQ4_XS quantizations to the SYCL backend (PR #31998). Published as a pre-release build.

    Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b12618

    2026-11-22

    5 items
    9B Q4_K_M / Q5_K_M / Q8_0 vision SenseNova

    SenseNova publishes official GGUF of SenseNova-Loop-9B

    Official GGUF of SenseNova's 9B image+text model (released 2026-11-17). It is a looped decoder that runs part of its layers twice, supports up to 512K context and is Apache 2.0. The model card points to OpenSenseNova's llama.cpp fork; upstream support is not stated.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/sensenova/SenseNova-Loop-9B-GGUF

    4B Q4_K_M〜IQ2_M bartowski

    bartowski publishes GGUF of Spark-X2.6-4B

    GGUF of XHToken's 4B model with 22 quantizations from BF16 to IQ2_M (Q4_K_M about 2.6 GB). It has a low/high thinking-effort setting, supports English and Chinese, and is Apache-2.0. A 1.7B GGUF is also available.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/bartowski/Spark-X2.6-4B-GGUF

    llama.cpp 新アーキテクチャ 8B (A1B)

    llama.cpp adds support for Zyphra's Zaya1 8B architecture

    PR #32071, merged on 2026-11-18, lets llama.cpp run Zaya1, a hybrid of CCA layers, standard attention layers and a top-1 routed MoE FFN (8B total / 1B active).

    Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/32071

    llama.cpp Vulkan b12612

    llama.cpp b12612: faster Vulkan MMVQ on AMD RDNA4

    Improves Vulkan quantized matrix-vector multiply for Q3_K, Q5_K and IQ4_XS on RDNA4 GPUs, keeping the default on AMD's proprietary driver, and cleans up shader loading (PR #31804).

    Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b12612

    llama.cpp 不具合修正 NVFP4

    llama.cpp b12611: fix missing scales in NVFP4 MoE experts

    Fixes NVFP4 MoE experts in some layers being copied before their per-expert scales were attached at load time, so they ran without scales (PR #32704).

    Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/b12611

    2026-11-21

    5 items
    8B (4B effective) Q4_K_M NVIDIA Unsloth

    Unsloth publishes GGUF of NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B

    A compact NVIDIA model built on the Gemma-4-E4B architecture: 8B total parameters (4B effective), about 4GB at Q4_K_M, up to 64K tokens in and out, with thinking mode toggled via the chat template. Licensed under OpenMDW-1.1.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/unsloth/NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-GGUF

    9B Q8_0〜Q3_K_L bartowski

    bartowski releases GGUF of MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B

    Quantized builds of a 9B Qwen-based distilled model, from Q8_0 (about 9.6GB) down to Q3_K_L; the common Q4_K_S / Q4_0 files are about 5.5GB.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/bartowski/MiMo-V2.6-Distill-Qwen-9B-GGUF

    4B / 9B vision inclusionAI bartowski

    GGUF builds of inclusionAI's vision models ZoomGain 4B / 9B

    inclusionAI released the image-text models ZoomGain-4B / 9B with official GGUF builds; bartowski also offers versions with 21 quantization types.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/collections/inclusionAI/zoomgain

    197M F16 / Q8_0 EN/RU

    Nano-1.3-Plus: a tiny 197M English/Russian chat model in GGUF

    Released November 15: a 197M-parameter model with 4,096-token context. F16 is about 512MB and Q8_0 about 274MB, small enough for phones or a Raspberry Pi 5. Meant for simple short chats; unreliable on facts and arithmetic.

    Source: https://huggingface.co/LGTEAMai/Nano-1.3-Plus-GGUF

    llama.cpp 1.0.0 ggml

    llama.cpp and ggml reach 1.0.0

    llama.cpp and ggml 1.0.0 were released on November 13, with API/ABI breaks to be signaled by major-version bumps from now on. Tags starting with b remain nightly builds; official releases use semantic-version tags.

    Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/blob/master/docs/release.md
