Updated 2026-11-27 (JST) · GGUF models up to 12B and llama.cpp updates

12B (MoE, 2.5B active) Q4_K_M IQ4_XS Q8_0 coding

bartowski publishes GGUF of JetBrains' 12B coding MoE Mellum2.1 Instruct

bartowski released imatrix GGUF quants of JetBrains' Mellum2.1-12B-A2.5B-Instruct (12B total / 2.5B active MoE, Apache 2.0), a non-reasoning instruct variant aimed at agentic coding. Quants range from IQ2_M (~5.4GB) to bf16, with Q4_K_M (~8.8GB) recommended; llama.cpp b12600 or newer is required.

Source: https://huggingface.co/bartowski/Mellum2.1-12B-A2.5B-Instruct-GGUF

llama.cpp release v1.1.0

llama.cpp v1.1.0 released

Following 1.0.0, the v1.1.0 semantic-version release tag was created on 2026-11-26 after the version bump to 1.1.0 (#33043); ggml was also bumped to 1.1.x around the same time.

Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/releases/tag/v1.1.0

llama.cpp Hexagon Snapdragon NPU

llama.cpp: ggml-hexagon 2.0 adds INT-HMX (W8A8) matmul and automatic F16 lowering (#32582)

The Hexagon (Snapdragon NPU) backend got a major overhaul: HMX W8A8 integer matmul (now the default mode), automatic F32-to-F16 lowering, generic DMA pipelines, Q8_0-KV flash attention, and F16/DMA optimizations across many ops including RoPE, softmax and SSM. The Snapdragon docs were updated too.

Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/commit/e0f884d4058d7acc117eb768a0a1c5290d0a8b94

llama.cpp OpenCL Adreno Android

llama.cpp: OpenCL adds Adreno A850 MoE int8 kernel and multi-token GEMV for speculative verify

Host-side support for an MoE int8 kernel targeting Adreno A850 was added (#33069), along with a multi-token F32 GEMV for the speculative-decoding verify step in the OpenCL backend (#33098, co-authored by a Qualcomm developer).

Source: https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/commit/47d6192fce301f627c4d3e1c6910588c364dce0e
